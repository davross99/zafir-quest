NEXT TO · NEAR · CLOSE TO
The ice cream shop is next to the cinema.beside something, with no space between
The supermarket is near* the cinema.a short distance away
The supermarket is close to the cinema.almost the same as near, but often a little nearer. Remember the to: close to the cinema.
BETWEEN
BETWEEN
Zafir is between Jack and Jia.in the space separating two people or things
IN FRONT OF · BEHIND
IN FRONT OF · BEHIND
Jia is in front of the tree.on the near side of something, closer to the person looking
Jack and Zafir are behind the tree.on the opposite side of something, further from the person looking
ABOVE · ON TOP OF
ABOVE · ON TOP OF
Zafir is floating above the box and the chest.higher than something, with a space between them
The box is on top of the chest.on the upper surface of something, touching it
OVER
There is a bridge over the river.directly above something and from one side of it to the other
Zafir holds an umbrella over Jack.directly above something and covering it
UNDER · BELOW
UNDER · BELOW
Jack is sitting under the umbrella.lower than something and covered by it
The boat is below the cliff.lower than something, but not always directly beneath it

* NEAR: say near the cinema, not near to the cinema. Near to is not wrong, but it is unusual, so English teachers usually say: no to after near. Close to always needs to.
