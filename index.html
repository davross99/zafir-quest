11 PREPOSITIONS OF POSITION
WHERE IS IT?
OPEN GAPS (space between)
ABOVE
Higher, with a gap
The lamp is above the table.
BELOW
Lower, with a gap
The river flows below the bridge.
UNDER
Directly beneath, touching
The cat is under the bed.
ON TOP OF
Higher, resting/stacked
The box is on top of the shelf.
HIDDEN / VISIBLE
BEHIND
Hidden by something
The key is behind the door.
IN FRONT OF
Facing / directly visible
Jack stood in front of the mirror.
DISTANCE
NEXT TO
Touching, side by side
The cup is next to the plate.
CLOSE TO
Very near, almost touching
She stood close to the window.
NEAR
A short distance away
The school is near the park.
SPANNING / BETWEEN
OVER
Across, high above
The bridge goes over the river.
BETWEEN
In the space separating two things
The box is between the two vases.
