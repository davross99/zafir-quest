Privacy Policy

App: Jack, Jia & Zafir's Quest
Developer: Games for Improving English (Zest English)
Last updated: 8 November 2026

Personal data

This app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party services.

Game progress

So that players can continue where they left off, the app saves their game progress (the current screen and score) only on their own device, using the browser's local storage. This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Players can delete it at any time by pressing Restart in the game or by clearing the app's data.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
